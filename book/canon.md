# THE WINDS OF KIRINA: CHRONICLES OF TIMBUKTU 2142 — CANON BIBLE

This file is the single source of truth. Every writer MUST use these names, dates, spellings and facts. Do not invent contradictory facts. You MAY invent new minor details (places inside a sector, minor NPCs, weapon variants, dialogue) as long as they do not contradict this file.

## 0. Tone & creative rules (from the creator)
- Genre: AAA open-world action-RPG with first/third-person shooter combat. Campaign mode (open world, branching, player choice drives the path, "Castlevania-like" progression from a start point where chosen paths reveal different factions) + a multiplayer battle-royale mode.
- Setting: a pseudo-historical, futuristic reimagining of Timbuktu, Mali. Grounded in Malian history and culture, loosely inspired by present-day political tensions — but ALL factions, people and events are FICTIONAL. Never name real living politicians, real armed groups, or real religions as villains. Faya's creed is an invented doctrine ("the Codified Word"), not Islam or any real faith.
- Visual direction: mud-brick (banco) Sudano-Sahelian architecture FIRST, futurism SECOND. Subtle circuitry, not neon cyberpunk. The creator explicitly asked to "tone down the cyberpunk". Avoid feeling like Fallout (no vault/wasteland clichés). Exploration should feel inspiring and interactive: floating scholar bastions, energy-harvesting monuments, shifting dunes that act as puzzles.
- Realism: the creator wants ideas grounded and concrete, not overly positive. Missions carry real cost and consequence. Choices are exclusive (the player picks ONE option, not all at once).
- Combat: heavy, visceral, weighty gunplay — "Wolfenstein-style" is the creator's reference for intensity (recoil, impact, destructible cover, brutal close quarters). Describe it in original terms.
- All in-world signage/background text is Latin script only.
- Ultimate goal of the story: liberate Timbuktu and REUNITE it with the greater Malian nation, restoring democratic, popular rule.

## 1. Title & naming
- Full title: **The Winds of Kirina: Chronicles of Timbuktu 2142**. Short: *The Winds of Kirina*.
- "Kirina" honours the Battle of Kirina (c. 1235), where Sundiata Keita's victory founded the Mali Empire — a story of unification. The game's theme mirrors it: a fractured land made whole.
- Campaign mode internal name: **The Sahara Rebellion**.
- Multiplayer mode: **Harmattan Royale** (the closing storm is a Harmattan sandstorm wall).

## 2. History & timeline
- c. 1235 — Battle of Kirina; Mali Empire founded.
- 1324 — Mansa Musa's pilgrimage; Timbuktu rises as a centre of learning (Sankoré, Djinguereber, Sidi Yahia). Manuscript culture.
- 2060s–2080s — "The Bright Decades": Mali becomes a solar and data power. Sun-harvesting monuments ("Sun Steles") and levitation lattices built over old Timbuktu. The manuscripts are digitised into the **Echo** — a historical datastream woven into the city's infrastructure.
- **2092 — The Fracture.** A power grab: a cabal of officers and financiers storms the Presidential Palace in Bamako. The state's systems collapse in a cascade ("the Breakdown"). The national grid splinters. Timbuktu is severed from the rest of Mali. Factions rise to fill the vacuum.
- 2092–2100 — The Scramble. J-Sector seizes the grid and water; Faya rises in the old religious-scholarly quarter by preaching order; MLI-Z declares the borderlands a free zone.
- 2101 — The **Azure Syndicate** (J-Sector's private security corporation) erects the neural blackout dampeners — surveillance and comms-jamming spires that cut Timbuktu off from Bamako.
- 2104 — Amadou and comrades found the **Kirina Resistance Front (KRF)** in the vaults beneath the Sankoré precinct.
- 2110 — Khalil born. 2115 — Awa born.
- 2139 — The Grid Famine: rolling blackouts; J-Sector sells power by the hour.
- **2142 (present) — The game begins.** Dr. Amadou leaves to retrieve the **Kirina Core** and never returns.

## 3. Places (Timbuktu 2142 and beyond)
The city is split into three faction sectors plus contested/open regions. Map orientation: Faya Sector = north/old city centre; J-Sector = south (toward the Niger river port of Kabara); MLI-Z Borderlands = the outer ring and desert to the east and north-east.
- **The Faya Sector (the Spires)** — the old scholarly/religious heart, mud-brick mosques-turned-towers. Faya has raised **transmission spires** that broadcast dogma day and night; they double as watch-posts. Maze-like alleys, oppressive. Glowing ORANGE circuit lines in the banco walls. Key sites: the **Spire of the Codified Word** (main energy/broadcast spire), the **Sanctuary of the Codified Word**, the Burned Library Courtyard, the Market of Silence.
- **J-Sector (the Craft & Data Quarter / Industrial Core)** — factories, smoking stacks, CYAN-glowing pipes and data lines, water channels, the **Data-Mining Cluster 7**, the **Generation Hall** (where the city's power cores sit), Textile Weaving Cluster, Traditional Metalwork District (exploited artisans), the river port of **Kabara**. HQ: the **Azure Citadel**.
- **MLI-Z Borderlands (the Free Zone)** — ochre/red earth, separatist outposts, fortified oasis farms, salvage yards, wind towers. Capital outpost: **Tin-Zawar**. Desert beyond is the **Erg of Echoes** — shifting dunes that rearrange (dynamic puzzle terrain).
- **Floating scholar bastions** — levitating academies over the city from the Bright Decades, half-abandoned. The most important: **the Bubble** (formal name: the **Aerostat Archive of Sankoré**) — a floating glass-and-banco sphere where Amadou was searching for the energy source.
- **Sun Steles** — energy-harvesting monuments; interactive puzzle objects.
- **The Foggara** — ancient underground water channels, extended into a tunnel network connecting sectors (the "Undercity").
- **The Well of Sankoré** — KRF headquarters in the vaults under the Sankoré precinct.
- **Kirina Deep** — a buried pre-Fracture installation far in the desert where the Kirina Core was hidden.
- Bamako — the capital, beyond the blackout, still the seat of a weakened but legitimate Malian national government waiting for Timbuktu's signal.

## 4. Factions
### J-Sector — the oligarchs
Corrupt, high-tech oligarchs who strip state resources: power, water, data, artisan labour. Present themselves as "keepers of order and progress". Leader: **Chairman Lassana Vey**, head of the **Board of Nine**. Enforcement arm: the **Azure Syndicate** — dark angular tech-armor, autonomous sentry turrets, Sentry Enforcers (heavy mechs), drones, gunships and fighter jets. They run the neural blackout dampeners. Most technologically advanced faction. Combat vs J-Sector = heavy, high-intensity firefights.
### Faya — the zealots
Religious fanatics enforcing an invented scripture, **the Codified Word**, written by their founder, **the First Voice** (deceased). They believe the Word "answers everything" and terrorise people into compliance; they dismantle cultural heritage (burn manuscripts, deface Bogolan, ban music). Population under Faya wears simplified, plain, slim, less intricate versions of turban-based attire (derived from Khalil's look but much plainer). Faya's military, the **Wardens of the Spire**, are regimented, disciplined, uniform — structured desert-ready plating, utilitarian riggings, protective wraps with traditional patterns (an imperial-trooper feel but culturally Sahelian, original design). Commander and first major villain: **Marshal Kessar Vohl** — a former national-army officer, extremely disciplined, augmented, heavily armed, a commanding and theatrical presence (original creation). Women and children are especially oppressed in the Faya Sector.
### MLI-Z — the separatists
Separatists seeking not just a free zone but a sovereign country of their own: they call it the **Free State of Tenzar**. Leader: **Commandant Assalan Teyr** — pragmatic, scarred, has real grievances (decades of neglect from Bamako, J-Sector water theft). MLI-Z is morally grey: a potential ally or enemy depending on player choice.
### Kirina Resistance Front (KRF) — the heroes' side
The "resistance military" — distinct from the other factions. Democratic/populist: wants elections, restored rights and reunification with Mali. Founded by Dr. Amadou. Field commander: **Colonel Mariam Diarra**. HQ: the Well of Sankoré. Insignia: a stylised wind-swept spear over a Bogolan chevron.
### Minor allied groups (recruitable, small)
- **The Griot Circle** — oral historians who "sing" the Echo; they can unlock historical datastreams.
- **The Azalai** — the salt caravan of Taoudenni; desert logistics, sand-skiffs.
- **The Ferrymen of Kabara** — Niger river boat clans (smuggling, river routes).
- **The Lantern Children** — orphan couriers and lookouts in the alleys.
- **Faya defectors ("the Unveiled")** — Wardens who lay down arms after the Faya liberation.

## 5. Characters
### Khalil — protagonist
- Age ~32. Son of Dr. Amadou. Mixed heritage: father from Ségou (Bambara), mother Tamasheq-speaking from the desert; he embodies the bridge between river and desert.
- Former desert scout for the KRF. Look: beige desert cloak and robes with intricate embroidered patterns and faint circuit-thread, **turban wrapped around his face — only his eyes visible**, brown leather armor accents (pauldron, belts, pouches), boots, **cybernetic LEFT arm** with exposed mechanics and wiring. Young, calm, relaxed face (not hardened).
- Weapons: **glowing cyan energy spear** (signature; throwable, returns via magnetic tether — the "spear throw" is iconic), **energy rifle** (side-slung). Combat role: tactical / heavy breacher and scout; versatile.
- Quote: "We will tear down their towers brick by brick until Mali is whole again."
### Awa — protagonist
- Age ~27. Khalil's cousin (Dr. Amadou is her uncle — her mother's brother). Darker skin than Khalil, slim, agile silhouette (gothic-hunter elegance). **Short braids** (shoulder length or shorter). Facial markings.
- Wears armor patterned in **Bogolanfini (mud cloth)** — black and bronze/brown geometric patterns over sleek cybernetic armor plates; mechanical arm augments. **Red-glowing energy sword** (machete-like blade). **Backpack rifle / gear pack** worn LOW on the back with a **plain, blank finish** (no Bogolan on the gear).
- Role: stealth, recon, tech-shaman. She "weaves" Bogolan runes through her gauntlet: rune patterns that hack energy spires, raise defense barriers, disrupt cloaks, tag targets with micro-drones. Precision anti-materiel sniping with the backpack rifle.
- Nickname: "Ghost of the Sahel". Quote: "The Syndicate built walls to hide their fear. We move through them like wind."
### Dusé — ally (joins in Act II)
- A man around thirty, a highly trained hacker and cyber-architect who has fought from the digital shadows for years to dismantle the dystopia and restore full democracy. **Pronouns: he/him.** His real name is never given.
- **Revealed late:** for most of the game he exists only as a voice, a violet relay glyph and remote hands on the city's systems. The player first sees his face in his Interlude II questline ("The Access Key"), when he chooses to meet the cousins in person.
- Look (portrait reference): short-cropped hair, athletic build, geometric tribal tattoos on the upper arms, a sleeveless vest with indigo/red/green woven geometric patterns, olive cargo trousers, worn combat boots; carries a translucent data tablet and a compact sidearm. Violet UI motif on his screens.
- Skills: remote turret subversion, neural override pulses, environmental grid hacks.
- Quote: "Power belongs to the citizens. I am merely rewriting the access key."
### Dr. Amadou — the missing father
- Look (portrait reference): elderly, white close-cropped beard, round glasses, embroidered indigo kufi cap, a long green boubou with geometric embroidery, sturdy boots; carries a leather-bound book and a brass magnifier.
- Age ~70. Born 2072; a young soldier during the Fracture; later historian, cryptographer and founder of the KRF. The bridge-figure who kept the resistance supplied with power and resources.
- 2142: leaves the Well of Sankoré to retrieve a power device — the **Kirina Core** — to sustain the resistance and free the city from J-Sector's grid. Never returns. Leaves encrypted grid bypass keys and a handwritten letter (in a Bogolan cipher) for Khalil and Awa.
- Truth (revealed late Act II): he reached the Bubble, decoded the Core's location (Kirina Deep), and was captured by the Azure Syndicate. Chairman Vey holds him in the Azure Citadel to force him to unlock the Core.
### Antagonists
- **Chairman Lassana Vey** (J-Sector) — final antagonist. Polished, rational, believes people cannot govern themselves.
- **Marshal Kessar Vohl** (Faya) — Act I boss.
- **Commandant Assalan Teyr** (MLI-Z) — rival/potential ally.
### Supporting
- **Colonel Mariam Diarra** (KRF commander).
- **Nana Kadia** — elder griot of the Griot Circle.
- **Moussa "Little Lantern"** — 11-year-old leader of the Lantern Children.

## 6a. Canonical story path (used for the pitch and The Complete Story)
Prologue → Act I Faya liberation → **Path of the Foggara** → Interlude I → Act II: Dusé → **Strategy Gamma (Broadcast Tower), alarms trip, air-raid breakout** → Operation Lantern → revelation → Interlude II (Dusé reveals his face) → Act III: **alliance with Tenzar** → Kirina Deep → Siege → **Reunification** ending. Other branches appear only as sidebars.

## 6. Campaign structure — The Sahara Rebellion
### Prologue — The Vanishing
Amadou disappears. Khalil and Awa find his empty vault, the bypass keys, and the **Letter** (Bogolan cipher). Decoding it reveals he went to **the Bubble** for the energy source. Open world unlocks. Investigation is player-driven: clues found through environmental hacking (primary), deciphering Bogolan ruins, and interrogating informants. Clues can lead to side missions or the main thread.
### Act I — The Spires (Faya Sector liberation)
- In the Bubble they learn they must "go elsewhere" — the trail runs through the Faya Sector.
- In Faya they witness despair: heritage dismantled, women and children oppressed.
- They exploit Faya's reliance on central energy spires: Awa plants a **tech-shamanic disruptor** at the base of the Spire of the Codified Word, overloading the grid. Suppression shields collapse; Khalil uses stealth to eliminate key snipers; Awa unleashes a data-surge to disable enemy armor; they fight back-to-back.
- Boss: **Marshal Kessar Vohl**.
- Reward: the **data link** → player choice:
  - **Path of the Drawer**: a key opens a drawer holding Amadou's **satellite-linked phone**, giving coordinates.
  - **Path of the Foggara**: the data link reveals an underground pathway (the Foggara) to another sector.
  Both converge in J-Sector, but with different allies, gear and enemy alert states.
### Act II — The Grid (J-Sector) 
- Meet **Dusé**. Player chooses ONE first strike (exclusive choice):
  - **Strategy Alpha — Central Data Heist** (stealth & cyber; leads Dusé & Awa): steal manifests proving J-Sector's neural subjugation and theft. Risk: firewalls trip → alert. Outcome: leaks spark regional unrest; public opinion swings.
  - **Strategy Beta — Convoy Ambush** (heavy vehicular; Khalil & Awa): seize Syndicate kinetic weapons in rocky canyons. Risk: collateral damage, gunship call-in. Outcome: arms the KRF for open siege.
  - **Strategy Gamma — Broadcast Tower Sabotage** (all-out; full squad; CRITICAL): break the neural blackout dampeners. Risk: if alarms sound, automated sentries fully encircle the ground perimeter and enemy fighter jets saturate the sky → desperate breakout under air bombardment. Outcome: restores communications, opens the path to reunification.
- **The Power Core mission ("Operation Lantern")**: the city's electrical grid is failing; the heroes must take a power core from the **Generation Hall** to restore power to the people. Requires penetrating J-Sector — the signature heavy, visceral firefight.
- Revelation: Amadou is alive in the Azure Citadel; Vey wants the Kirina Core.
### Act III — The Winds (Borderlands, Kirina Deep, Siege)
- MLI-Z: negotiate (alliance with Tenzar on terms of autonomy within a reunited Mali) or fight.
- Kirina Deep: retrieve the **Kirina Core** before the Syndicate.
- **The Siege of Timbuktu**: combined assault — traditional desert warfare tactics + cyber-espionage; rescue Amadou from the Azure Citadel; confront Chairman Vey; ignite the Core; broadcast to Bamako.
- Endings (based on accumulated choices & reputation):
  1. **Reunification** — Timbuktu rejoins Mali; elections announced; Tenzar autonomy negotiated.
  2. **The Fragile Accord** — city free but divided; reunification pending.
  3. **The Iron Peace** — Syndicate broken but KRF rules by force; Khalil/Awa question what they became.
  4. **The Silent Wind** (fail state) — the Core is lost; the resistance scatters.

## 7. Game systems (canon terms)
- **Echo Layer / Echo Sight**: environmental hacking overlays historical datastreams on the modern ruins (holographic reconstructions of historic libraries, scholars, markets). Puzzles and lore. Central to cultural identity.
- **Rune-weaving** (Awa): Bogolan rune patterns as hack/ability inputs.
- **Spear-link** (Khalil): spear throw + magnetic recall; can pin, overload nodes.
- **Combat**: heavy recoil, armor deformation, destructible banco and concrete cover, electrical conduits that spark/detonate, metal doors that buckle under breaching.
- **Team combos**: e.g., Dusé hijacks a turret + Awa vent shot + Khalil breach.
- **Reputation / Accord meter**: standing with KRF, populace, MLI-Z, Griot Circle, Azalai, Ferrymen, Lantern Children, Unveiled.
- Character swap between Khalil and Awa in the open world; Dusé as remote support.
- Open-world traversal: on foot, **sand-skiffs** (hover sleds), Azalai caravans, river pirogues.

## 7b. Ascension (powered-up forms)
- Concept art shows figures with glowing eyes holding orbs of light: these are **Ascension** forms, powered-up states of the playable characters, not separate characters.
- Source: **Echo Seeds**, orbs of condensed historical datastream. The first fragment is found in the Bubble (Act I) and gives a brief, unstable taste; full Ascension unlocks after the Kirina Core is recovered at Kirina Deep (Act III).
- Built by **Resonance** (earned through Echo hacks, team combos and protecting civilians). When Ascended, the eyes glow amber and an Echo orb forms in the palm; abilities are amplified for a short window.
- Forms: Khalil — *Wind of Kirina*; Awa — *Mother of Runes*; Dusé — *Ghost Signal* (remote, city-wide). Exact abilities are defined in Part V.
